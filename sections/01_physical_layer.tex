\section{Physical interface and service placement}

Each DAPHNE provides one dedicated 1\,GbE SFP connection for CCM, as specified
in the DAQ--PDS ICD [R1, R2]. All board-facing services below share this link:
\begin{center}
\textbf{DAPHNE Ethernet interface $\longleftrightarrow$ switch
$\longleftrightarrow$ physical server.}
\end{center}
PDS supplies the fibres to its mini-rack patch panels; continuation and active
switching are coordinated with DAQ and Technical Coordination [R1].

Services may share a physical host. The installation record shall name their
hosts, board/switch/server ports, SFPs and patch routes. OPC UA terminates on
the external server. Board services, configuration and monitoring share the
available GbE bandwidth.
