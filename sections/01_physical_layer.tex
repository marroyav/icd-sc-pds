\section{Physical interface and service placement}

Each DAPHNE provides one dedicated 1\,GbE SFP connection for CCM, as specified
in the DAQ--PDS ICD [R1, R2]. Its board services share this link:
\begin{center}
\textbf{DAPHNE Ethernet interface $\longleftrightarrow$ switch
$\longleftrightarrow$ physical server.}
\end{center}
PDS supplies the fibres to its mini-rack patch panels; continuation and active
switching are coordinated with DAQ and Technical Coordination [R1].

The PDS Calibration Box (Light Calibration Module, LCM) has its own dedicated
1\,GbE SFP CCM connection [R2]. PDS PoF laser boxes connect through their laser
controllers; their Ethernet medium, rate and connector remain to be specified.
Both use the switched control network to reach the external physical server.

Services may share a physical host. The installation record shall name their
hosts, device/switch/server ports, media and patch routes. OPC UA terminates on
the external server. DAPHNE services share each board's available GbE bandwidth;
calibration and PoF controllers have separate network endpoints.
