\section{Responsibilities}

PDS shall define equipment operating limits and protection requirements.
DPS shall implement the agreed protection functions and publish their status.
SC shall enforce operational permissions and publish detector availability and
activity information. DAQ shall consume that information for run admission and
data tagging.

Protection functions shall reference a versioned cause-and-effect matrix
specifying the input source, protected equipment, action, response time,
actuator feedback, failure behavior and reset conditions. Protective action
shall remain effective independently of DAQ and the SC bridge.
Section~\ref{sec:proposed-matrix} contains the proposed PDS matrix.

PDS provides photon detector and light calibration hardware, firmware, native
services for the warm electronics, detector specifications and detector operating restrictions. It shall
provide monitoring information sufficient for SC to assess and confirm detector
health and diagnose readout network connections, including SFP
I\textsuperscript{2}C diagnostics.

SC provides control and monitoring infrastructure, arbitrates access to detector
controls and monitoring data, and assesses and publishes detector status.
DPS provides protection infrastructure.

DAQ uses the detector, configures it, collects its data and provides calibration
datasets agreed in advance with PDS. It shall check the status published by SC
and take data only when SC advertises detector availability.

\textbf{Ops arbitration (proposed; ownership to be agreed).} SC completes the
handshake with Operations (Ops) and advertises detector availability only after
detector health and Ops authorization are confirmed.
