\section{Responsibilities}

\begin{center}\small

\textbf{Group} & \textbf{Role} \\
\midrule
PDS & Provides photon detector and light calibration hardware, firmware, native services, detector specifications and detector operating restrictions. \\
SC & Provides control and monitoring infrastructure, arbitrates access to detector controls and monitoring data, and assesses and publishes detector status. \\
DPS & Provides protection infrastructure and detector protection. \\
DAQ & Uses the detector. Shall check the detector status published by SC and complete a handshake with Operations (Ops) before taking data. Configures the detector, collects detector data and provides calibration datasets agreed in advance with PDS. \\
