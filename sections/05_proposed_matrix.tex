\section{Proposed PDS interaction matrix}
\label{sec:proposed-matrix}

\textbf{Hardware interlock.} Automatically trips or inhibits affected equipment
through hardwired, local supply or autonomous DPS protection. It enforces an
approved safe state independently of DAQ and the SC bridge.

\textbf{Operational constraint.} Governs permitted activities and state
transitions through SC/Ops coordination. It may deny or hold a conflicting
request; an operational conflict alone does not justify an equipment trip.
Permitted overlaps are recorded with data-quality tags.

The following proposals retain the source matrix rule IDs [R9]. Each rule
requires agreement by PDS, the affected subsystem and the responsible
SC/DPS/DAQ owners.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{L{0.27\textwidth}L{0.20\textwidth}L{0.46\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Interaction / rule} & \textbf{Class} & \textbf{Proposed response} \\
\midrule
Purity monitor / Q-001 & Data quality & Allow acquisition; SC publishes activity intervals and affected region for DAQ tagging. \\
Camera illumination / O-001 & Operational exclusion & Deny the conflicting request; flag unexpected overlap and alert Ops. \\
Ionisation or alignment laser / O-002 & Operational exclusion & Deny the conflicting request; require emission/shutter-off confirmation before PDS return. Flag unexpected overlap and alert Ops. \\
LCM calibration / Q-002 & Calibration & Allow declared calibration; publish activity, configuration identity and affected scope for DAQ tagging. \\
PoF / C-001 & Power dependency & Required for cathode PDS. Apply independent protection for its own faults. \\
Supply current or voltage fault / P-011 & Equipment protection & Trip or inhibit the affected supply through the approved local/DPS path; verify its final state. \\
Equipment overtemperature / P-012 & Equipment protection & Apply the approved local/DPS action to affected power, readout or calibration equipment. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The activity rules assign no PDS bias trip. Personnel laser safety remains
independent. PoF has no DAQ interface.

\textbf{Open for owner agreement:} excluded PDS operating states; membrane and
cathode scope; limits and response times; input validity and failure behavior;
actuator mapping and feedback; settling, reset and recovery conditions.
