\section{Physical interface}

\subsection{DAPHNE}
\label{sec:physical-daphne}
Each DAPHNE provides one dedicated 1\,GbE SFP connection for CCM, as specified
in the DAQ--PDS ICD [R1, R2]. Its board services share this link:
\begin{center}
\textbf{DAPHNE Ethernet interface $\longleftrightarrow$ switch
$\longleftrightarrow$ physical server.}
\end{center}
PDS supplies the fibres to its mini-rack patch panels; continuation and active
switching are coordinated with DAQ and Technical Coordination [R1].

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Detector} & \textbf{PDS channels} & \textbf{Channels/board} & \textbf{DAPHNE boards} \\
\midrule
FD-VD & 1,344 & 32 & 42 \\
FD-HD & 6,000 & 40 & 150 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These are the detector design allocations [R2], with one CCM connection per
board. FD-VD includes membrane and cathode modules; both use this CCM interface.


The installation record shall identify board/switch/server ports, SFPs and
patch routes. All DAPHNE CCM services share the board's GbE bandwidth.

\subsection{Light Calibration Module}
\label{sec:physical-calibration}
\textbf{Inventory under control and monitoring:} 8 LCMs for FD-VD and 18 for
FD-HD (planning quantities; PDS confirmation pending).

Each Light Calibration Module (LCM) has a dedicated 1\,GbE
SFP CCM connection [R2]: LCM Ethernet interface $\longleftrightarrow$ switch
$\longleftrightarrow$ physical server. PDS shall specify the installed
detector mapping, ports, media and patch routes.

\subsection{PDS PoF laser boxes}
\label{sec:physical-pof}
\textbf{Inventory under control and monitoring:} 80 laser boxes for FD-VD
(design allocation [R7]); none for FD-HD.

Power-over-Fiber (PoF) supplies the FD-VD cathode modules [R2]. A compact PLC
system controls and monitors the laser boxes. The interface to the control
network is Ethernet. PLC placement, laser-box I/O connections, Ethernet medium,
rate, connectors and patch routes remain to be specified. PDS and DPS shall
define the physical PLC/protection connection.
