\begin{abstract}
This document defines the control, configuration and monitoring (CCM) and
detector protection interfaces for DAPHNE, the Light Calibration Module and the PDS Power-over-Fiber
(PoF) laser boxes. Each hardware system has its own physical-interface,
service and protocol description. The interfaces cover PDS, Slow Controls (SC),
the Detector Protection System (DPS) and DAQ where applicable. DPS provides
protection infrastructure and detector protection.

Configuration and control use request/response; monitoring is published.
The scope includes SC control and monitoring, DAQ use of DAPHNE and LCM,
and DPS protection interfaces.
DAQ data readout transport and optical timing transport are outside this scope.
\end{abstract}
